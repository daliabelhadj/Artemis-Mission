🚀 Artemis Mission

*"What would you do if you woke up alone on an alien planet, with no ship, no team, and no way home?"*

Artemis mission is a 2D game developed with Unity, featuring animated NPC dialogues, coin and health tracking, a timer, win/lose conditions, and a final score screen.

## Gameplay

- Travel through **Planet 1, Planet 2 and finally the Moon!**.
- Run, jump on platforms and collect coins.
- Find a gun mid-level, collect bullets and shoot enemies.
- Meet an alien guide who appears in each level and tells the story of the planet.
- **Boss fight:** shoot the boss's 3 glowing weak points in the correct order and use the Nova Blast at the right moment for double damage.

**Win:** reach the Moon, defeat the beast and save your friends.
**Lose:** fall off the map or get killed by enemies.

## Features : 

- Main menu with Play and Quit buttons
- Keyboard movement (left/right) and jumping
- Coin collection with a live counter
- Shooting system with collectible ammo
- Enemies and a final boss fight
- NPC dialogue system with text animation
- Lives (hearts), timer and score displayed on screen
- Final score screen with a Play Again option
